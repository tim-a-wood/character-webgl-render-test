This package contains a modified reference derived from Einar: static mesh extraction, material baking and portable PBR conversion, Draco compression, source-curve strand conversion, approximate view-facing strand shading, sampled facial deformation and corrective combinations, one source-sampled wrist gesture, and selective colour/roughness-metallic texture compression. No endorsement by the original authors is implied.

The original Blender source and rig are not included. The runtime model is not a complete export of the source rig. Four facial controls and one sampled wrist gesture are provided. Ear/nose hair and face/hat fuzz are absent. Source shader fidelity remains approximate.
