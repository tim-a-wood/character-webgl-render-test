Known limits

This page reuses the exact tested iteration 60 runtime and all 41 motion keys. It adds only an optional measurement interface; no speed gain is claimed. The art-directed walk adjusts the neck by −18° and head by −16°, keeping chest, pelvis and stride unchanged. Existing whole-character controls, appearance, camera and lighting are retained. Prior body interpolation and facial/groom composition limits remain. The clip is finite, with no loop claim. Physical-device frame rate is unmeasured.
